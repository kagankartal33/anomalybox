## Frequently asked questions

### What must Audience intent mapping decide first?

Set one clear default for **audience intent mapping** next to **3-pillar framework**. State the exact condition that justifies a change. A before-and-after decision is far more useful than a long reference deck because you can test it directly on set.

### How can 3-pillar framework be tested with simple gear?

Protect the proof moment above all else. Cut optional shots first. A short version works well when **3-pillar framework** stays visible and claims stay inside your approved boundary.

### When should Analytics review be updated?

Update **analytics review** when repeated passes on **shoot-ready shot lists** show the same friction. A shift in audience, offer, or weekly capacity also justifies an update. A single slow video is not a reason to rebuild your whole system.
