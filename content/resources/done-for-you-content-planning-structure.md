## Frequently asked questions

### What must Pillar allocation decide first?

Set one clear default for **pillar allocation** next to **batch filming system**. State the exact condition that justifies a change. A before-and-after decision is far more useful than a long reference deck because you can test it directly on set.

### How can Batch filming system be tested with simple gear?

Protect the proof moment above all else. Cut optional shots first. A short version works well when **batch filming system** stays visible and claims stay inside your approved boundary.

### When should Performance tracking be updated?

Update **performance tracking** when repeated passes on **asset repurposing** show the same friction. A shift in audience, offer, or weekly capacity also justifies an update. A single slow video is not a reason to rebuild your whole system.

### How long should this workflow take for Done-For-You Content Planning Structure for Growth Creators?

Aim for 45 minutes total production for **Done-For-You Content Planning Structure for Growth Creators**. Spend 15 minutes setting up your mic and camera, film 3 takes, and confirm a before-and-after decision before tearing down.
