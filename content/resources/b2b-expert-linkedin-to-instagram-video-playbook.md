# The B2B Video Playbook: Turn Tech Knowledge into Short Video

For consultants, founders, and agency operators, video can feel risky. Many B2B leaders see Reels and LinkedIn video as shallow tools for jokes or trends.

Yet the B2B buyer path has shifted. Key buyers, fund partners, and leaders watch short video feeds daily. When an expert explains a real business problem in 45 seconds, they earn trust fast. They win deals with less friction.

This guide gives you a clean plan to turn domain skills into short videos that win enterprise clients.

## Quick answer

To build a trusted B2B video engine:
1. **Use the Case Breakdown format:** Turn a 5-page case study into a 45-second story. Show the hidden block, the fix, and the metric result.
2. **Use eye-level directing:** Frame chest-up with a 50mm lens. Keep the backdrop clean. Keep your voice calm.
3. **Drive to a high-ticket call:** Skip generic like-and-comment pleas. Invite qualified peers to request your full brief.

## The B2B Video System: The 45-Second Case Study

```text
0:00 - 0:03: The Industry Bottleneck (Hook)
0:03 - 0:15: The Common Mistake vs The Reality
0:15 - 0:35: The 2-Step Fix (Screen / Diagram Proof)
0:35 - 0:45: The High-Status Next Step (CTA)
```

### 1. The High-Stakes Problem (0:00 – 0:03)
Open on the exact pain your buyer faces: *"If your SaaS sales cycle takes over 60 days, your pricing page is leaking pipeline here."*

### 2. The Failed Industry Dogma (0:03 – 0:15)
Contrast common tips with facts: *"Most firms say hire more reps. But in 20 sales audits, adding reps cut deal size by 28%."*

### 3. The Concrete Fix (0:15 – 0:35)
Show the real sheet or system on screen. Point right at the core block.

### 4. The Executive Next Step (0:35 – 0:45)
Close with calm words: *"If you are scaling past $5M ARR and want to audit your pipeline, send a message with the word 'Audit'."*

## Directing Comparison: B2B Authority vs. Consumer Creator

| Directing Dimension | Consumer Creator Style | B2B Authority Style |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Vocal Energy** | Fast, loud, hyper | Calm, clear, steady |
| **Camera Framing** | Handheld selfie, moving | Locked tripod, chest-up, 50mm |
| **Background / Set** | Busy room or street | Minimalist office with warm light |
| **Supporting Proof** | Meme cuts and sound effects | Real data tables and screen clips |
| **Primary Metric** | Vanity view counts | High-value inbound calls and closed deals |

## The 3 High-Impact B2B Video Formats

### Format 1: The Postmortem Breakdown
Break down a failed test or bad campaign. Showing why a tactic failed proves real skill. It builds deep trust.

### Format 2: The 60-Second Framework
Put a 3-part plan on screen. Walk through each step in under one minute.

### Format 3: The Deal Breakdown
Share a real story from a client deal. Reveal the hidden objection and how you solved it.

## Fill-in-the-Blank B2B Script Formula

```text

+ [Contrarian Proof]: "In our last client audit with a [company type], we found that [surprising metric finding]."
+ [The 2-Step Fix]: "Instead of [bad habit], fix it in two steps: First, [Step 1]. Second, [Step 2]."
+ [High-Status CTA]: "If you are scaling right now, comment '[KEYWORD]' and I will send our full brief."
```

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Will short video hurt my reputation as a senior advisor?
No. High-level buyers respect concise, high-density explanations. They prefer a 45-second clear video over a 40-page whitepaper.

### Does this strategy work on LinkedIn as well as Instagram?
Yes. Short video is now the fastest-growing organic reach format on LinkedIn. It generates 3x to 5x more views than text posts alone.
