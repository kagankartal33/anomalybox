## Frequently asked questions

### What must Assemble for meaning first decide first?

Set one clear default for **assemble for meaning first** next to **cut on action**. State the exact condition that justifies a change. An audience question answered on screen is far more useful than a long reference deck because you can test it directly on set.

### How can Cut on action be tested with simple gear?

Protect the proof moment above all else. Cut optional shots first. A short version works well when **cut on action** stays visible and claims stay inside your approved boundary.

### When should Complete a final safety pass be updated?

Update **complete a final safety pass** when repeated passes on **use text as navigation** show the same friction. A shift in audience, offer, or weekly capacity also justifies an update. A single slow video is not a reason to rebuild your whole system.

### How long should this workflow take for A Calm Creator Editing Workflow?

Aim for 45 minutes total production for **A Calm Creator Editing Workflow**. Spend 15 minutes setting up your mic and camera, film 3 takes, and confirm an audience question answered on screen before tearing down.
