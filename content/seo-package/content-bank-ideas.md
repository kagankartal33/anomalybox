# Content Bank Ideas: Store Concepts at the Right Level of Detail

A useful response to **content bank ideas** is a system that stores more than titles. It records why an idea matters, what evidence is available and whether the creator can make it under real conditions.

## Quick answer

Build a bank that stores tension, angle, proof, production mode and status — not just titles. Review weekly, promote a few ideas to briefs, and archive duplicates so the bank reduces decisions instead of multiplying them.

## Design the bank

Ideas are being stored without enough context to become usable. Store each entry with audience tension, proposed angle, source, proof, content job, production mode, expiry date and status.

## Use a simple status flow

```text
Captured → Reviewed → Selected → Briefed → Produced → Published → Learned
```

Do not let every captured note enter production. Review on a fixed schedule and archive duplicates or ideas that cannot be responsibly supported.

## Apply the next actions

1. Save the audience tension, angle, proof and source.
2. Tag by lane and content job.
3. Score by usefulness and production fit.
4. Promote ideas into briefs weekly.
5. Delete duplicates and expired trend notes.

## Define a minimum strategy

For **Content Bank Ideas: Store Concepts at the Right Level of Detail**, keep one audience, two or three content roles, one primary platform and a cadence that can survive a constrained week. The minimum should still provide real value; it simply removes optional production layers.

## Preserve sustainability

For **Content Bank Ideas: Store Concepts at the Right Level of Detail**, measure the cost of the system as well as its reach. Include production time, recovery, revision load, qualified conversations and contribution to the business. Stop activities that create persistent cost without meaningful value.

## Fields every bank entry needs

| Field | Why it matters |
| :--- | :--- |
| Audience tension | Prevents random clever titles |
| Angle | One sentence point of view |
| Proof available | Stops ideas you cannot support on camera |
| Production mode | Solo / UGC / faceless / desk |
| Effort score (1–5) | Protects capacity |
| Status | Captured → Selected → Briefed → Done |

## Weekly bank ritual (30–40 minutes)

1. Capture 5–10 tensions from comments, DMs, support tickets or client questions.  
2. Score each for usefulness and filmability.  
3. Promote only 2–3 into brief cards.  
4. Archive expired trends and duplicates.

A bank that never deletes becomes another overwhelm machine.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a spreadsheet enough?

Yes, if it is used. The fields and review habit matter more than the tool.

### Should old ideas be deleted?

Delete duplicates and expired notes. Keep useful observations that can be refreshed with a new example or source.

### How many selected ideas should be active?

Only the number you can brief and produce in the next planning cycle. Too many active ideas recreate the original decision problem.
