## Frequently asked questions

### Should I follow trending idea lists?

Use them as observation inputs, not ready-made concepts. A trend needs a relevant audience tension, a distinct example and accessible proof.

### How many ideas belong in the bank?

Enough to offer choice without creating a second backlog. Quality of context matters more than count.

### What if none of the ideas feel exciting?

Choose usefulness and production fit. Excitement often returns after a clear, manageable piece is completed.

### What is the first decision to make for i don't know what to post on instagram?

Name the audience tension and the proof you can show on camera this week. Ideas without proof stay stuck in notes.

### How do I avoid copying other creators?

Keep the mechanism (tension → proof → payoff). Change the surface: your props, language, location and product truth.

### When is a finished shoot brief worth buying?

When you can explain the strategy but still freeze on shot order, spoken lines or claim boundaries every week.
