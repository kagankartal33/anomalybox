## 🎬 What is Anomaly Box?

[Anomaly Box](https://anomalybox.com) eliminates decision fatigue for short-form video creators. Instead of generic AI text prompts, creators receive structured, locked shoot briefs delivered directly into their secure digital vault.

Each brief settles:
- **The Visual Hook (0:00–0:03):** High-tension opening visuals and spoken lines.
- **Scene-by-Scene Flow:** Exact order of shots with physical action and camera framing.
- **On-Screen Text & Captions:** 3-word dynamic cues designed to maintain retention.
- **Anti-Claim Boundaries:** Explicit warnings on what *not* to claim on camera.
